# {frontmatter.title}

Finding safe drinking water is an important part of any trip, especially when you are camping. If you are travelling in a campervan, motorhome or caravan, you should also consider where, when and how to refill your fresh water tank.

In this guide, you will find answers to common questions about drinking water in Finland.

## Is tap water safe to drink?

Tap water is safe to drink throughout Finland. Drinking water supplied by public water utilities is treated and monitored to meet health requirements. It is uncommon to find a tap with a sign stating that the water is not suitable for drinking.

However, it is always a good idea to use common sense. If you come across a tap that looks dirty or are unsure where the water comes from, check with the staff or the property owner before drinking it.

## Can you drink water from Finnish lakes and streams?

If you are planning to spend several days in nature during your trip, you may wonder whether lake water is safe to drink. Finland has many lakes and streams with clear-looking water, but appearance alone does not tell you whether the water is safe.

Some outdoor enthusiasts drink untreated lake water, but this is not generally considered safe, even when the water looks very clean. If you need to collect drinking water from a lake or stream, boil it or use a suitable water filter or purification method. Avoid water that shows signs of blue-green algae or is suspected of being polluted, and find another source of drinking water.

## Where can you fill reusable water bottles?

It is usually easy to refill your reusable water bottles at taps in places such as cafés, restaurants and fuel stations. Refilling a reusable bottle is an easy way to save money and reduce the need to buy single-use plastic water bottles during your trip.

## Where can you refill your fresh water tank?

One of the best options for refilling your campervan, motorhome or caravan's fresh water tank is to stay at a campsite for a night. Even if you prefer wild camping, it is best to manage your fresh water and wastewater at designated facilities.

Many overnight parking areas for campers, known in Finnish as _matkaparkki_, also offer fresh water refilling and wastewater disposal, often at a lower nightly rate than a full-service campsite.

### Water refill points at Finnish fuel stations

You may also find water taps outside some fuel stations, such as ABC service stations. These may have an outdoor cabinet or tap marked _vesi_, which means water in Finnish. When it is not clear that the tap is intended for campervans and caravans, it is good practice to ask the staff if you can use the tap to refill your fresh water tank and if there is a charge. The same applies to wastewater disposal facilities at fuel stations.

As always, it is a good idea to bring your own clean water hose and the necessary adapters for refilling your tank. A clean watering can is also useful if you cannot connect a hose directly to the tap.
