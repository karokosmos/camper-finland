# {frontmatter.title}

Wild berry picking is one of the easiest ways to experience Finnish nature. During summer and early autumn, Finnish forests are full of berries that you can pick and enjoy for free.

If you are travelling around Finland by campervan, motorhome or caravan, berry picking is also an easy activity to add to your road trip. If you have a freezer in your vehicle, you can pick bilberries, lingonberries or other berries and enjoy them later during your trip.

In this guide, you will find information about when and where to pick wild berries in Finland, what equipment to bring, and what to keep in mind when picking berries in Finnish forests, bogs and other natural areas.

## Everyone can pick wild berries in Finland

Everyman's rights generally allow everyone, including foreign visitors, to pick wild berries in Finland without asking the landowner for permission. There is no fee for picking berries for your own use.

However, everyman's rights come with responsibilities. You must not cause damage or disturbance, and special rules can apply in national parks, nature reserves and other protected areas. Keep a distance from homes and stay away from gardens, cultivated fields and plantations.

## The best time to pick berries

The berry season in Finland usually runs from mid-July to late September, but the timing depends on the type of berry, your location and the weather.

In general, the berry season starts earlier in southern Finland and later in Central Finland, Northern Finland and Lapland. A warm summer can make berries ripen earlier, while a cold summer can delay the season.

## How to find wild berries

You can often find wild berries simply by exploring the forests and other natural areas around you. You can also use maps and apps such as Karttaselain to help you find areas where different berries are likely to grow.

Karttaselain is available in English and has maps showing the typical habitats of bilberries, lingonberries and cloudberries. These maps can help you identify promising areas, but they are only estimates, so they do not guarantee that you will find berries in a particular spot.

## Popular berries to pick

### Bilberries

Bilberries (often called blueberries or wild blueberries in everyday English) are perhaps the most popular wild berry in Finland and are usually easy to find. Just head to the nearest forest, and there is a good chance you will find them. You can eat them as they are, add them to yoghurt or porridge, or put them on pancakes.

Bilberries are usually ready to pick from mid-July to early September. The exact timing depends on the location and weather. In northern Finland, the season can start later. They grow throughout Finland, especially in forests.

#### Bog bilberries

Bog bilberries look similar to bilberries and can sometimes be picked by mistake. They look and taste a little different, but they are also edible, so there is no need to worry if you find some among your bilberries.

### Lingonberries

<Image src={lingonberryImg} alt="Lingonberries in Finnish forest" class="rounded mt-0 mb-0" />

Lingonberries are another popular berry, especially if you like more acidic or sour flavours. They are also relatively easy to find.

Lingonberries are usually ready to pick from mid-August onwards, and the season can continue into October. They grow throughout Finland, especially in dry and open forests.

### Cloudberries

<Image src={cloudberryImg} alt="Cloudberries in Finnish forest" class="rounded mt-0 mb-0" />

Cloudberries are a highly valued wild berry with a distinctive flavour. They can be more difficult to find than bilberries or lingonberries.

Cloudberries are usually ready to pick in mid-July and August, depending on the location. They grow mainly in swamps, bogs and other wet areas, and are particularly common in northern Finland.
